KNOW WHAT YOU'RE LOOKING AT

Confidence should earn your trust.

What the percentage means

An 80% prediction range aims to contain a future daily price about 8 times out of 10. It is not an 80% chance that the central estimate is exactly right. “Chance of cheaper” estimates the probability of a price below the latest observed rate on that specific day.

Built from observed prices

The central forecast blends damped five-day momentum, a pull toward the current season's recent price level, and a small weekday effect. Ranges and cheaper-price estimates use errors from historical forecasts at the same horizon. Wipe-crossing outcomes are excluded.

Tested in time order

Every historical estimate uses only observations available at the time. Coverage tests also calibrate ranges only on errors already known then. A short history and overlapping seven-day tests mean these figures are preliminary, not independent trials.
